A notice and what this is

This is my technical blog. I am not a professional in either of the subjects I wrote about in this blog.

As these are mostly things I did for projects or had to learn about, you probably could find far better resources out there. The only things that might help someone are the niche subjects I had to beat my head against, for which there were little to no resources at the time. Those are very few, though. I probably whined about them at the beginning of the post, so you can easily guess which of the posts I am talking about.

That being said, this blog represents a way for me to index things I enjoyed, things that frustrated me, or things that I simply found curious.
